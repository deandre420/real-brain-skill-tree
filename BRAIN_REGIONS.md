# Brain Regions and Functions

## Overview
This document provides detailed information about the major regions of the human brain, their functions, and interconnections. This serves as the knowledge base for the Brain Skill Tree visualization.

## Major Brain Regions

### 1. Frontal Lobe
**Location**: Front of the brain
**Primary Functions**:
- Executive functions and decision making
- Problem solving and planning
- Personality expression
- Motor control
- Speech production (Broca's area)
- Impulse control and emotional regulation

**Sub-regions**:
- **Prefrontal Cortex**: Executive functions, personality, decision making
- **Primary Motor Cortex**: Voluntary movement control
- **Premotor Cortex**: Movement planning
- **Broca's Area**: Speech production

**Connections**: Strong connections to parietal lobe, temporal lobe, limbic system

---

### 2. Parietal Lobe
**Location**: Upper back portion of the brain
**Primary Functions**:
- Sensory processing (touch, temperature, pain)
- Spatial awareness and navigation
- Mathematical reasoning
- Body position awareness (proprioception)
- Visual-spatial processing

**Sub-regions**:
- **Primary Somatosensory Cortex**: Touch and sensory processing
- **Superior Parietal Lobule**: Spatial awareness
- **Inferior Parietal Lobule**: Language and mathematical operations

**Connections**: Frontal lobe, occipital lobe, temporal lobe

---

### 3. Temporal Lobe
**Location**: Sides of the brain (near temples)
**Primary Functions**:
- Auditory processing
- Memory formation and retrieval
- Language comprehension (Wernicke's area)
- Facial recognition
- Emotion processing

**Sub-regions**:
- **Primary Auditory Cortex**: Sound processing
- **Wernicke's Area**: Language comprehension
- **Hippocampus**: Memory formation and consolidation
- **Amygdala**: Emotion processing, fear response

**Connections**: Frontal lobe, parietal lobe, limbic system

---

### 4. Occipital Lobe
**Location**: Back of the brain
**Primary Functions**:
- Visual processing
- Color recognition
- Motion detection
- Depth perception
- Visual memory

**Sub-regions**:
- **Primary Visual Cortex (V1)**: Basic visual processing
- **Visual Association Areas (V2-V5)**: Complex visual processing

**Connections**: Parietal lobe, temporal lobe, thalamus

---

### 5. Cerebellum
**Location**: Below the occipital lobe, at the back of the brain
**Primary Functions**:
- Motor coordination and balance
- Fine motor control
- Motor learning
- Cognitive functions (attention, language)
- Timing and rhythm

**Connections**: Brainstem, motor cortex, sensory systems

---

### 6. Brainstem
**Location**: Base of the brain, connecting to spinal cord
**Primary Functions**:
- Basic life functions (breathing, heart rate)
- Sleep-wake cycles
- Alertness and arousal
- Relay of sensory and motor information

**Sub-regions**:
- **Midbrain**: Vision, hearing, motor control, alertness
- **Pons**: Sleep, respiration, swallowing, bladder control
- **Medulla Oblongata**: Heart rate, breathing, blood pressure

**Connections**: Cerebellum, spinal cord, cerebral cortex

---

### 7. Limbic System
**Location**: Deep within the brain
**Primary Functions**:
- Emotion processing and regulation
- Memory formation
- Motivation and reward
- Olfaction (smell)
- Behavioral responses

**Key Structures**:
- **Hippocampus**: Memory formation and spatial navigation
- **Amygdala**: Emotional processing, fear, and aggression
- **Hypothalamus**: Hormone regulation, temperature, hunger, thirst
- **Thalamus**: Sensory relay station
- **Cingulate Cortex**: Emotion formation, learning

**Connections**: Frontal lobe, temporal lobe, brainstem

---

### 8. Corpus Callosum
**Location**: Center of the brain
**Primary Functions**:
- Communication between left and right hemispheres
- Coordination of bilateral movements
- Information integration

**Connections**: All cortical regions of both hemispheres

---

### 9. Basal Ganglia
**Location**: Deep within the brain
**Primary Functions**:
- Motor control and movement initiation
- Procedural learning and habit formation
- Reward and motivation
- Executive functions

**Key Structures**:
- **Striatum**: Movement control, reward processing
- **Globus Pallidus**: Movement regulation
- **Substantia Nigra**: Dopamine production, movement control

**Connections**: Frontal cortex, thalamus, brainstem

---

## Hemispheric Specialization

### Left Hemisphere (typically)
- Language production and comprehension
- Logical reasoning
- Mathematical and analytical thinking
- Sequential processing
- Right-side body control

### Right Hemisphere (typically)
- Spatial awareness
- Facial recognition
- Artistic and musical abilities
- Holistic thinking
- Left-side body control
- Emotional processing

---

## Neural Pathways and Networks

### Default Mode Network (DMN)
- Active during rest and introspection
- Involved in self-reflection, memory, and planning
- Includes: medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate cortex, precuneus

### Salience Network
- Detects and filters important stimuli
- Switches between DMN and task-positive networks
- Includes: anterior insula, dorsal anterior cingulate cortex

### Executive Control Network
- Active during complex cognitive tasks
- Problem-solving and decision-making
- Includes: dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, posterior parietal cortex

---

## Development and Plasticity

### Brain Development
- **Early Childhood**: Rapid growth, synapse formation
- **Adolescence**: Pruning, frontal lobe development
- **Adulthood**: Continued plasticity, experience-dependent changes
- **Aging**: Some decline, but continued adaptation

### Neuroplasticity
- Brain's ability to reorganize and form new connections
- Enhanced by learning, practice, and novel experiences
- Recovery mechanism after injury
- Basis for skill acquisition and memory

---

## Clinical Significance

### Common Brain Disorders by Region

**Frontal Lobe**:
- ADHD, dementia, personality changes, motor disorders

**Temporal Lobe**:
- Epilepsy, memory disorders, auditory processing disorders

**Parietal Lobe**:
- Sensory processing disorders, spatial neglect, calculation difficulties

**Occipital Lobe**:
- Visual processing disorders, cortical blindness

**Cerebellum**:
- Ataxia, balance disorders, coordination problems

**Limbic System**:
- Anxiety, depression, PTSD, memory disorders

**Basal Ganglia**:
- Parkinson's disease, Huntington's disease, Tourette's syndrome
